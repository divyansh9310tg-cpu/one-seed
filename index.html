01Seed

Every summer begins asleep.

Inside one small striped seed: a root, a stem, a hundred petals, and a thousand more seeds. It only needs dark, water, and time.

02Awaken

Roots before reaching.

Underground, in the dark, the shell splits. The first thing a seed does is anchor itself. Only then does it climb.

03Reach

Everything up toward light.

The stem hooks through the soil, straightens, and unfolds its leaves. Each one is a tiny solar panel, aimed at the sun.

04Bloom

For one week, it opens.

A sunflower is not one flower but hundreds: petals on the outside, tiny florets in a golden spiral within.

05Ripen

Gold turns into seed.

The petals fall. The head bows under its own weight. Every floret in the spiral swells into a striped seed, and the spirals count in Fibonacci numbers.

06Again

It ends where it began.

A few seeds fall. One lands in the soil. The cycle never finishes, it only hands over. Keep scrolling and it begins again.
